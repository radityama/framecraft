# Optional creative starting points

These are judgment aids, not mandatory animation presets. Use the production brief and the existing HTML authoring API. Each template needs its own references, asset rights and reading-time checks.

| Direction | Composition and type | Motion and timing | Events and sound | Review emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tech documentary | Evidence first: real code, diagrams, provenance and restrained captions; type separates explanation from evidence. | Establish context, show the mechanism, then hold the consequence. Motivate pans across connected evidence; cut instead of animating every label. | Reveal, connection, state change, cut. Sparse contact/paper accents; optional ambience only if authorized. Voice is opt-in. | Are claims sourced? Can the viewer follow the mechanism without narration? Are diagrams legible at full speed? |
| Desktop simulation | Coherent desktop, active/inactive windows, real task content, correct clipping, focus and z-order; local UI sans. | Approach, settle, press, release, response. Preserve grip through drag and allow reading/confirmation pauses. Camera follows attention sparingly. | Press/release, key, grab, friction, drop, transition. Dry material sounds with variants; silence between actions. | Cursor hotspot/targets, causal frames, drag geometry, stacking, focus, temporal plausibility and sound scale. |
| Product explainer | One concrete user task and outcome; show the actual product or an explicitly labeled simulation. | Orient → act → observe result. Use a stable spatial anchor, stage reveals around the action, hold the result. | Meaningful interaction/result, not decorative chatter. Sound supports cause and feedback; no automatic music or voice. | Is the task truthful and understandable? Do demonstrations match claimed behavior? Can labels be read? |
| Editorial graphics | A strong text/image hierarchy, deliberate negative space and consistent caption system; cited factual content. | Cut on ideas; transitions preserve a relevant relationship. Vary shot scale and pacing, not every element's easing. | Page turn, annotation, underline, cut. Paper/contact texture only where justified; quiet for reading. | Argument, sources, typography, continuity, pauses and excessive motion competing with text. |
| Data story | Explicit scales, units, sources and annotations. One comparison at a time; no decorative or misleading axes. | Establish baseline before change. Preserve identity/axis through updates; pause at meaningful comparisons. | Data change, highlight, threshold. Restrained accents can direct attention; don't sonify every point by default. | Correct values, perceptual honesty, baseline continuity, reading time, and whether sound implies unsupported significance. |
| Cinematic type | Exact approved words, a purposeful typeface, confident line breaks and controlled scale/negative space. | Stage phrase structure; anticipation and follow-through fit the material. Protect reading holds; camera does not constantly drift. | Word/phrase reveal, cut, impact or release. Sound belongs to emphasized ideas, with dynamic contrast and silence. | Exact wording, glyphs, line breaks, hierarchy, pace, restraint and whether accents overwhelm meaning. |

For every direction: use the versioned beat/event plan and cue sheet; inspect boundary/milestone frames; record named Gate B observations and limits; complete Gate A independently; obtain Gate C only where required. Deliberate silence is allowed with an explicit reason. A template never authorizes narration, music, external assets or automatic approval.
