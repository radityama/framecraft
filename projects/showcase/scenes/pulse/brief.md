# Pulse / the mark

An editorial motion study of one ink impression becoming a wave. 960 × 540, 30 fps, 96 frames. `opening` occupies scene frames 0–47; `echo` occupies 48–95. Cut with no overlap.

- `opening`: a small vermilion disc takes its place in an offset paper composition, then becomes the focal point. Inspect local 0, 24 and 47.
- `echo`: the same mark sends expanding rings across the sheet, from a tightly held first frame to a broad last frame. Inspect local 0, 24 and 47.
- The low hum loops across the cut; the opening tap and the second tap overlap it. Audio is diagnostic, not a supplied soundtrack.

The limited ink palette, serif title and open paper space evoke a printed instrument plate. The ring is a measurement trace, not decorative background texture. Every state is computed from the requested shot-local frame.
